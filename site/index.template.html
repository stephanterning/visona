Beat-synced scope & spectrum analysis

See the groove,
not just the waveform.

Visona draws your audio as a sweep scope locked to the bar. Run it as a standalone app on macOS or Raspberry Pi, with audio from your interface and optional MIDI Clock sync. Or use the plugin inside your DAW.

Locked to the beat

MIDI Clock sync with Start, Stop, Continue and Song Position Pointer. As a plugin it follows the DAW's transport directly. FREE mode runs at a manual BPM.

Sweep scope

Bar grid, stacked left and right lanes and a bright write head. Windows from a quarter bar to four bars, horizontal zoom and a measurement ruler.

Read the frequency balance

Choose Spectrum (FFT) for a fast Peak or RMS curve with a fading history line, octave smoothing and a frequency readout, or Spectrum (Band) for responsive third-octave bars with peak markers. Both use a black background; the fill and the bars follow your waveform colour. FFT supports stereo, Left, Right, Mid and Side.

In the next release, after 1.0.0 beta 1.

See the stereo image

Polar Sample and Lissajous views reveal channel relationships; Polar Level summarizes the angular level distribution, with correlation and energy balance alongside. Silence and very weak signals are marked as unavailable. Common display gain changes the picture without changing the measurement.

In the next release, after 1.0.0 beta 1.

Three waveform modes

STD, PRECISE and DJ, where colour follows frequency. Display gain up to 64 dB, with optional auto gain.

Latency you can see

Visona Sync puts a bar impulse on the plugin's sidechain, so Visona measures how late the audio arrives after plugins with latency, and draws it on the grid.

Your own studio display

Run Visona standalone on Raspberry Pi with a screen and an audio interface. Keep your scope beside your gear, with full-screen kiosk mode, touch controls and automatic startup. No DAW needed on the Pi.

A standalone app for your Mac

Open Visona directly on macOS and monitor your audio interface without a plugin host. Follow MIDI Clock or set your own tempo. Available for Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or later.

Inside your DAW, too

Use Visona as a plugin that follows your host transport: VST3, AU and CLAP on macOS, and VST3 and CLAP on Windows and Linux.

Open source

Written in C++ with JUCE and released under the AGPL-3.0. Built and checked on GitHub Actions for every release.

Standalone for macOS

Download Visona-macos-arm64.zip, unzip it and move Visona.app to Applications. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Standalone setup.

From 1.0.0 beta 1 on, the app and plugins are signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens them without warnings. Plugin bundles go into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, Components or CLAP; see the manual.

Windows and Linux

VST3 and CLAP plugins for Windows x64 and Linux x86_64. Install folders are listed in the manual.

Standalone for Raspberry Pi

Download Visona-linux-arm64-pi.tar.gz for Raspberry Pi OS 64-bit with the desktop, based on Debian 13 (Trixie) or later. Add a screen and an audio interface for a dedicated studio display. Standalone setup. See Pi setup and kiosk mode, or install over SSH with install-visona.sh.
